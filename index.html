Overlay Board

Upload a data file or program PDF. Overlay Board lays out the whole card: past performances, scratches, money moves, and where the board likes a horse.

1 Bring in your data

Brisnet data file

Brisnet’s Single File export, or a multi-part card (the four-file .DRF/.DR2/.DR3/.DR4 set some tracks ship). A .zip is fine and is opened for you — for a multi-part card send the whole zip, since the four files only work as a set. If .drf looks greyed out in the picker, rename it to .csv; the parser reads the contents, not the name.

Program PDF

Brisnet-style past-performance sheet. Runs in the browser; if it hangs on a phone, use Paste text instead.

Paste text

Open the PDF, select all, copy, paste here. Same extraction as the PDF option and works everywhere.

2 Check & edit entries

ML odds comes in with the file. Final starts empty — fill it in at the track; it never overwrites the morning line. Surface fit and Class are optional. Tick Scr for a scratch and rebuild the card: scratched horses drop out and the field renormalizes.

Apply scratches from the Equibase feed

Load your entries first, then choose the track’s Changes & Scratches feed or paste it below. Scratches are ticked automatically by race and program number. Rider changes are flagged on the card, with the new rider’s meet record where the file has one.

★ Where the board likes a horse

Two separate lines, and the only place this tool names a horse. Pick — the market favourite also holds the field's single highest average of its last three speed figures. Price play — a separate line, used only where a Pick is not available: the morning-line favourite is second in the field on career earnings per start.

🖨 Printable page

The whole card on one page — picks, race cards and full field. Use your browser's own Print or Share from here.

3 Race card — every race, in race order

Which column the math reads. Final odds falls back to the morning line for any horse you haven't updated, so you can fill in one race at a time.

4 Full field, race by race

always in post order
How to read this: odds are converted to an implied win probability and normalized across the field to strip out the track's built-in edge. The tool does not know track bias, late scratches, trip trouble or today's pace shape — mark scratches in the Scr column, rebuild, and read the past performances yourself before betting.
